Releasing What No Longer Fits

Tuesday, September 15 • 7:00–8:30 p.m. Eastern • Live on Zoom

Sometimes we know something no longer fits long before we are ready to let it go.

A role we have carried.

An expectation we have lived beneath.

A way of being that once helped us feel safe, needed, accepted, or loved.

Even when we begin to recognize these things, releasing them is not always simple.

Because what no longer fits may once have served us well.

Releasing What No Longer Fits is an invitation to notice, with compassion rather than judgment, what you may be ready to carry differently — or perhaps begin laying down.

Together, we’ll gently explore the roles, expectations, patterns, and old ways of being that may have belonged to another season of your life.

You do not need to arrive ready to make a change.

You do not need to know what you are ready to release.

Sometimes simply recognizing what feels heavy is enough to begin.

In this gathering, we’ll make space to explore:

  • What you may be carrying that no longer feels like yours
  • The roles or expectations that have become heavy
  • What an old pattern may once have protected or provided
  • What it might feel like to loosen your hold, without forcing yourself to let go


There will be time for quiet reflection, gentle conversation, and meaningful connection with other women walking their own path of return.

Sharing is always an invitation, never an expectation.

A place to pause.
A place to notice.
A place to loosen your hold.
