Listening Again

Tuesday, August 18 • 7:00–8:30 p.m. Eastern • Live on Zoom

There are seasons when we become so accustomed to listening to everyone and everything around us that we can barely hear ourselves.

The needs.
The expectations.
The responsibilities.
The voices telling us what we should do, who we should be, and what should matter.

Your own.

Listening Again is an invitation to slow down enough to notice what has been waiting beneath the noise.

Together, we’ll gently explore what it means to hear yourself again — your feelings, your needs, your desires, and the quiet inner knowing that may have been there all along.

You do not need to arrive with answers.

You do not need to know what comes next.

You are simply invited to come as you are.

In this gathering, we’ll make space to explore:

  • What you may be noticing about yourself right now
  • Where outside voices or expectations may have become louder than your own
  • What your inner knowing has been quietly trying to tell you
  • One small way you might begin trusting what you hear

There will be time for quiet reflection, gentle conversation, and meaningful connection with other women walking their own path of return.

Sharing is always an invitation, never an expectation.

A place to pause.
A place to listen.
A place to remember yourself.
