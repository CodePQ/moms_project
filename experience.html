Return to You Experience

A quiet space to pause, reflect, and reconnect with yourself.

A complimentary invitation for the women of The Quiet Return community.

There are seasons when life is full of responsibilities, people to care for, and things that need our attention. And somewhere in the middle of it all, we may begin wondering: Where am I in all of this?

The Return to You Experience is an opportunity to spend a little time together and listen to what you may have been noticing in yourself. Over approximately 75 minutes, I’ll gently guide us through four movements: REMEMBER → RETURN → CHOOSE → STAY.

There is nothing to prepare. You don’t need to have read A Quiet Return, arrive with answers, or share anything you would rather keep to yourself. Simply come as you are.
